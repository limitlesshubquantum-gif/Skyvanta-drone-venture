About the Project

SKYVANTA is a drone technology venture being developed under SYDEX – Student Entrepreneurship Network.

Our project explores innovative drone-based delivery solutions designed to transform last-mile transportation of groceries and everyday essentials.

Our Vision

To contribute to the future of intelligent aerial transportation through innovative, reliable and accessible drone technology.

Our Concept

Aerial Delivery

Exploring drone-based delivery of groceries and everyday essentials.

Smart Technology

Exploring smartphone-based control and intelligent drone systems.

Future Logistics

Investigating new possibilities for efficient last-mile delivery.

Our Initiative

SKYVANTA is being developed as a project under SYDEX, a student entrepreneurship network encouraging innovation, entrepreneurship and practical problem-solving.
